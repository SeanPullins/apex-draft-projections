## Draft-slot-free accuracy benchmark (not an achieved new result)

The private APEX v19 retrospective study includes a **no-draft-pick** baseline. For the *2015–2018 evaluation subset* (921 eligible observations), it reports:

| Four-year NFL outcome | No-pick Brier | No-pick AUC | Market / pick-dependent Brier |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Positional top-quartile four-year AV (`hit4`) | **0.1840** | **0.6762** | 0.1570 |
| At least two NFL seasons with 8+ starts (`starter4`) | **0.2098** | **0.6915** | 0.1719 |

These are different feature-information regimes and not an apples-to-apples incumbent replacement. The report itself flags previously explored draft classes, backfilled (rather than contemporaneously frozen) features, and potential identity issues. A new prospect-first model must beat the *no-pick* benchmark on identical draft-year-eligible players using vintage-valid features; include prospective held-out seasons, calibration and position slices. **No accuracy gain from Muse can be claimed yet.**

Source: private `nfl-prospect-dataset-private/reports/apex_v19/summary.json`, `hit4` and `starter4` → `2015+`. No raw privately licensed stats reproduced here.
